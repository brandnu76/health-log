# 4-week starter plan (kettlebells + bench)

**Started:** 2026-10-09 · **Runs:** weeks of Oct 12 – Nov 6, 2026 · **Review:** Saturday after week 4

Brandon: male, 50, 5'7", about 200 lb. On Wegovy (semaglutide) max dose since 2026-03-16. Not training before this plan. Lean mass has been sliding (muscle mass 132.5 lb on Mar 7 → 125.4 lb on Oct 3).

## Goal

Get training going again so the body has a reason to keep muscle during the cut. These 4 weeks are about showing up, learning the movements, and building a logging habit. Hard training comes later. Three easy, consistent sessions a week beat one hard session followed by a missed week.

## Schedule

- **Mon / Wed / Fri, about 5:15 PM**, right after getting home and before unwinding or starting dinner.
- **About 25 minutes** per session: 3–5 min warm-up, about 20 min of lifting.
- Same session every day (below). Missed a day? Do it the next day. Don't double up.

## Equipment

- Adjustable kettlebells: **25 / 30 / 35 / 40 lb** (5 lb steps)
- Flat bench

## Warm-up (3–5 min)

1. Bodyweight squats: 10–15, slow
2. Arm circles: 10 forward, 10 back
3. Hip hinges (hands on hips, push hips back, flat back): 10–15
4. One light first set of the goblet squat (for example 25 lb × 5). Mark it as a **warm-up** set in Hevy.

## The session

Use these **exact Hevy exercise names** every time. A renamed exercise starts with no history.

| # | Hevy exercise name | Sets × reps | Starting weight (suggested) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Goblet Squat (Kettlebell)` | 3 × 8–10 | 25–30 lb |
| 2 | `Bench Press (Kettlebell)` | 3 × 8–10 | 25 lb per hand with two bells; otherwise one arm at a time. Floor press is fine if the bench is awkward |
| 3 | `One-Arm Row (Kettlebell)` | 3 × 10 per side | 30–35 lb |
| 4 | `Romanian Deadlift (Kettlebell)` | 2 × 10 | 35–40 lb (one bell in both hands, or two bells) |
| 5 | `Plank` | 2 × 20–30 s | Bodyweight |

- **Rest:** 60–90 s between sets.
- **Tempo:** take **3 seconds to lower**, pause briefly, then lift under control. No bouncing.
- **Effort (reps in reserve, RIR):**
  - Weeks 1–2: stop about **3 reps short of failure (RIR 3)**. It should feel like you could do 3 more good reps.
  - Weeks 3–4: about **2 reps short (RIR 2)**.
- The starting weights are a guess. If the first set is too easy or too hard, change the weight by one step and log what you actually used.
- If you're short on time, do every exercise and cut one set from the RDL. Don't skip a whole exercise.

## Progression (double progression)

1. Stay at one weight until **every set hits the top of the rep range** (10 reps; 10 per side on the row) with good form and the 3 s lowering.
2. Next session, go up **5 lb** and start back at the bottom of the range (8 reps).
3. **When 40 lb gets too easy** (it will on the RDL and maybe the row), there's no heavier step, so progress in this order:
   - add reps, up to **15**
   - add a **4th set**
   - slow the lowering to **4 s**
   - add a **2 s pause** at the bottom
4. Plank: add 5 s per set once 30 s is easy. Cap at 45 s.

## Safety (Wegovy, age 50)

- **Don't lift fasted or dehydrated.** Wegovy can cause lightheadedness, and the 7:30–4:30 workday is long. Drink water in the afternoon and have a glass before you start.
- **Small protein 1–2 h before or right after:** a scoop of whey in water (about 25 g protein) works and barely registers as food.
- **Stop the session if you feel dizzy, faint, short of breath, or have chest pain.** Sit down, drink water, and don't finish the workout. Tell your VA clinician if it happens again.
- **Neutral spine** on the RDL and row: push the hips back, keep the bell close to the legs, no rounding. Stop the set when form slips, even if you planned more reps.
- **Joint or heart issues:** get your clinician's okay before going heavier.
- Pain in a joint (not muscle burn) means skip that exercise that day and note it in Hevy.

## Protein swaps (no extra food)

Wegovy keeps you full, so the plan **swaps foods instead of adding them**:

- **Plain Greek yogurt instead of regular yogurt** at breakfast: about twice the protein in the same container.
- **Whey in water on lifting days:** about 25 g protein, small volume.
- Keep the ½ cup cottage cheese at breakfast.
- Daily protein target stays at the protocol's range. Cronometer will show whether the swaps get there.

## Logging (Hevy)

- Log every session in Hevy as you go, using the exact exercise names above.
- In each exercise's **notes**: the kettlebell setting (and whether one bell or two) and tempo, e.g. `30 lb, 1 bell, tempo 3-1-1, RIR 3`.
- Mark warm-up sets as **warm-up** so they don't count toward volume.
- **Saturday:** after the Fitdays scan, send the Council the Hevy export (or a screenshot of the week). It gets logged per [README.md](README.md).
